# Spidertron Chores

A Factorio 2.1 (Space Age) mod: spidertrons do their own chores. Pick jobs per spider in a panel next to its window.

- **Defend**: the nearest ready spider runs to attacks on your walls, turrets and buildings, fights from range, and comes back when it's quiet.
- **Restock when low**: walks to its restock point (home, or e.g. your main factory) for ammo and repairs, then goes back.
- **Restock turrets**: tops up gun, rocket and artillery turrets near home.
- **Build outposts**: works like construction bots where your roboports don't reach, inside its work area and only with items it has or can fetch. Also handles upgrades, deconstruction and rebuilding.
- **Lamps**: puts lamps around buildings near home, with connected poles where it's dark.
- **Clear nests** near home while healthy and stocked.
- **Patrol**: walks a saved waypoint route or a ring of guard posts around home.
- **Shuttle**: ferries items between two spots using two logistic request sections.

Home, restock point and shuttle stops can be set where the spider stands or picked anywhere on the map.

## Install

Zip this folder as `spidertron-chores_<version>.zip` (the zip should contain a `spidertron-chores_<version>` folder) and drop it into Factorio's `mods` folder. Version history is in `changelog.txt`.
